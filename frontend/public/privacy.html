Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

In short: MakeCalgaryTalk needs very little about you. Your location is used only to work out which community you are in, and the coordinates are never stored. We never ask for your home address. Other players see your nickname, your community and what you helped build on which day — never the time.

Who we are

MakeCalgaryTalk (makecalgarytalk.com) is an independent game made by a Calgary developer. It is not affiliated with The City of Calgary or Calgary Transit. Questions or requests: hello@makecalgarytalk.com.

What we collect and why

  • A player account. When you first open the game we create an anonymous account on your device so your progress is kept. If you choose to link Google, we receive the email address and basic profile Google shares, used only to sign you in and keep your progress.
  • Your villager. The nickname and how your villager looks (age, skin tone, hair, clothes and colour) as you choose them. Both are public in the game.
  • Your community. When you check in, your browser shares your location once; our server checks which Calgary community that point is in and then discards the coordinates. We store only the community and the day of each check-in (needed for the 3-check-ins-in-7-days resident verification).
  • What you do in the game. Your daily actions (which project, which day), invites you sent or accepted, community posts you write, thanks and reports, and your answers to today's question. Posts are public in your community; for today's question only the counts are shown, never who chose what.
  • Usage counts. To see whether the game works for people we record, per day, that you visited, which communities you looked at, and when you shared a card or opened an invite link. No times, no device fingerprints, no advertising IDs. Page views are also counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.
  • Email updates (optional). Only if you tick the box in your profile: your email address and when you agreed. Untick it anytime and we delete the address (we keep the date you unsubscribed as proof).
  • Bot protection. Sign-in uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which looks at technical signals from your browser to tell people from bots.

What other players can see

Your nickname, how your villager looks, community, the day you helped a project (for example “Sam helped the skating rink”), badges, and posts you write. Never your location, your check-in times, your email or your Google account.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — database and sign-in (our data is hosted in Canada).
  • Cloudflare — website hosting, bot protection (Turnstile) and cookie-free page statistics.
  • Google — only if you choose “Link Google”.

We do not sell your information, and we do not show third-party advertising based on it.

How long we keep it

As long as your account is used. You can ask us to delete your account and everything linked to it at hello@makecalgarytalk.com; we will do so within 30 days. Community totals that never identified you (for example how many buildings a community built) stay.

Age

MakeCalgaryTalk is for people aged 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 is using it, contact us and we will delete the account.

Your rights

Under Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act and Canada’s PIPEDA you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw consent. Write to hello@makecalgarytalk.com.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the game before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
