Credits

Data, sounds and software MakeCalgaryTalk is built with

Map and community data

  • The City of Calgary Open Data — community district boundaries, community population, and hydrology (lakes, ponds and rivers). Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – City of Calgary.
  • OpenStreetMap — CTrain lines and stations, and the parks, hills, creeks, ponds and schools suggested for each community. © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Islands are playful, simplified versions of each community, not accurate maps.

Weather

  • Environment and Climate Change Canada — Calgary's current weather on the islands. Data source: Environment and Climate Change Canada, used under the Environment and Climate Change Canada Data Servers End-use Licence.

Music and sounds

  • “Life of Riley” and “Jingle Bells (Calm)” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0.
  • “Wind.Synth 3.Winter(3osc)” by newlocknew, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  • All other sounds are in the public domain (CC0), with thanks to their authors on Freesound (vdr3, florianreichelt, steaq, NickTayloe, theplax, WIM, rollingmill, Exchanger, Nox_Sound, Qvantor, xkeril, 7778, Vrymaa, melakoma, Valenspire, Fission9, TRP, craigsmith, egomassive, csnmedia), Kenney, OpenGameArt and SpritePilot. Sounds were trimmed and their volume adjusted.

Software

  • React, three.js, react-three-fiber and drei, Zustand, Tailwind CSS (MIT licences).
  • Supabase (database and sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting and Turnstile bot protection).

MakeCalgaryTalk is not affiliated with The City of Calgary or Calgary Transit. “CTrain” is used only to name the real transit lines shown in the game.
